## Baza Usług Rozwojowych — BUR

### What is Baza Usług Rozwojowych?

**Baza Usług Rozwojowych**, usually abbreviated as **BUR**, is Poland's national Development Services Database administered by the **Polish Agency for Enterprise Development — Polska Agencja Rozwoju Przedsiębiorczości (PARP)**.

The database contains development services such as:

* vocational training
* professional courses
* workshops
* advisory services
* postgraduate education
* coaching
* mentoring
* other competence-development services

BUR allows individuals and organisations to find registered training providers and development services, including services that may be financed with public or European funding.

Official information:

[Baza Usług Rozwojowych](https://uslugirozwojowe.parp.gov.pl/)

---

### Is BUR itself a funding programme?

No.

This distinction is important.

**BUR is a national database and development-services system.**

The actual subsidy normally comes from a specific national, regional or European programme administered by a funding operator.

A typical process may therefore involve:

1. identifying an appropriate funding programme
2. checking whether the participant or organisation is eligible
3. contacting or applying to the relevant operator
4. selecting an eligible service in BUR
5. following the operator's registration and financing procedure
6. completing the service according to the programme rules

Finding a Bensari Workshop course in BUR does not itself create an automatic entitlement to public funding.

---

### Who administers BUR?

Baza Usług Rozwojowych is administered by the **Polish Agency for Enterprise Development — PARP**.

PARP operates the national database, while particular subsidy programmes may be administered by different regional or national operators.

## Krajowy Fundusz Szkoleniowy — KFS

### What is Krajowy Fundusz Szkoleniowy?

**Krajowy Fundusz Szkoleniowy (KFS)** — the National Training Fund — is a separate part of the Polish Labour Fund used to support continuing education of working people.

Its purpose is to support professional competence and qualification development and to reduce the risk of people losing their position in the labour market because their skills no longer correspond to changing economic needs.

Official information:

[Krajowy Fundusz Szkoleniowy — Ministry of Family, Labour and Social Policy](https://psz.praca.gov.pl/dla-pracodawcow-i-przedsiebiorcow/podnoszenie-kompetencji-i-kwalifikacji-pracownikow-i-kandydatow-do-pracy/krajowy-fundusz-szkoleniowy)

---

### Who can benefit from KFS under the rules applicable in 2026?

KFS supports continuing education of working people.

The current framework includes:

* employees
* employers
* people conducting sole proprietorships
* people performing work under eligible civil-law contracts

The training must be undertaken on the initiative of or with the consent of the entity applying for KFS support.

Individual eligibility must still be assessed under the current regulations and the particular KFS recruitment call.

---

### Must a KFS training provider be registered in BUR in 2026?

This is one of the significant changes introduced together with the new labour-market legislation and the closure of RIS.

Bensari Workshop is registered in BUR.

---

### Is BUR funding the same as KFS funding?

No.

**BUR and KFS are not synonyms.**

BUR is the national development-services database and provider system.

KFS is a separate public financing mechanism forming part of the Labour Fund.

The systems are connected because KFS-funded training must now be provided by a provider meeting the relevant BUR registration requirement.

---

### How much can KFS finance in 2026?

Under the rules applicable in 2026:

* a **one-person entity or an entity employing no more than 9 people** may receive up to **90% of eligible continuing-education costs**
* other eligible entities may receive up to **70% of eligible continuing-education costs**

The applicant therefore contributes its own funds toward the cost.

The amount awarded may be lower than the maximum percentage.

---

### Is there a maximum KFS amount per participant?

Yes.

KFS financing for one participant in a calendar year cannot exceed **200% of the average remuneration** applicable for the relevant calculation.

This means that even where a percentage calculation would give a larger amount, the statutory participant limit still applies.

---

### Are there annual KFS limits for an applicant?

Yes.

Under the 2026 framework, the annual amount available to one applicant is linked to the size of the organisation.

The maximum annual limits are:

* up to **4 times the average remuneration** for entities with no employees or employing up to 9 people
* up to **8 times the average remuneration** for entities employing 10–49 people
* up to **12 times the average remuneration** for entities employing 50–249 people
* up to **14 times the average remuneration** for entities employing more than 249 people

These are maximum statutory limits.

They do not guarantee that the full amount will be awarded.

## Military reconversion and woodworking training

### What is military reconversion in Poland?

**Military reconversion** is a system of assistance intended to help eligible professional soldiers and former professional soldiers prepare for civilian professional activity.

The system can include:

* vocational information
* vocational counselling
* vocational retraining
* job placement assistance
* professional practice
* reimbursement of certain eligible costs connected with retraining

The objective is to help the participant acquire or develop competences that support employment or professional activity outside the armed forces.

---

### When can a professional soldier use vocational retraining assistance?

Under the current general statutory framework, a professional soldier may use assistance in the form of vocational retraining and job placement **during the six months before discharge from professional military service**, provided the soldier has completed at least **four years of professional military service**.

Different forms of reconversion assistance may have different time conditions.

Vocational retraining should therefore not be confused with professional practice or general vocational counselling.

---

### Can a former professional soldier use vocational retraining after leaving the military?

Potentially, yes.

An application for vocational retraining assistance can generally be submitted within **two years from the date of discharge from professional military service**.

Eligibility can depend on the circumstances in which the person left professional military service.

The competent military authority should therefore confirm the participant's individual entitlement.

### How long can military vocational retraining last?

The statutory system limits the duration of assistance in the form of vocational retraining.

Under the applicable framework, vocational retraining may not continue beyond the period permitted by the legislation governing the reconversion process.

A participant should confirm the exact implementation timetable with the competent military authority when submitting an application.

### How is the military reconversion training limit calculated?

The amount available for vocational retraining is linked to the **lowest basic salary of a professional soldier**.

The statutory base limit for vocational retraining is calculated as:

**60% of the lowest basic salary of a professional soldier.**

The level of the participant's entitlement then depends on length of military service.

Because military salary levels may change, the PLN value of the limit should always be checked for the relevant year.

---

### What is the military reconversion training limit in 2026?

In 2026 the lowest basic salary of a professional soldier is **PLN 6,500 gross**.

The basic vocational retraining limit calculated at 60% is therefore:

**PLN 3,900**

The amount that can be covered for an individual depends on length of military service.

---

### How does length of military service affect reconversion funding?

Under the current general rules, the vocational retraining entitlement is:

* after at least **4 years of military service** — **100% of the limit**
* after at least **9 years of military service** — **200% of the limit**
* after at least **15 years of military service** — **300% of the limit**

Using the 2026 base limit of PLN 3,900, this corresponds to:

* after 4 years — up to **PLN 3,900**
* after 9 years — up to **PLN 7,800**
* after 15 years — up to **PLN 11,700**

These are statutory maximum retraining amounts based on the 2026 salary-related limit, not an automatic entitlement to payment of the full course cost.

## Terminology

### BUR

**Baza Usług Rozwojowych** — Poland's national Development Services Database administered by the Polish Agency for Enterprise Development.

---

### PARP

**Polska Agencja Rozwoju Przedsiębiorczości** — Polish Agency for Enterprise Development.

PARP administers Baza Usług Rozwojowych.

---

### KFS

**Krajowy Fundusz Szkoleniowy** — the Polish National Training Fund.

KFS is part of the Labour Fund and supports continuing education of working people.

---

### PUP

**Powiatowy Urząd Pracy** — District Labour Office.

District Labour Offices administer KFS applications at local level.

---

### RIS

**Rejestr Instytucji Szkoleniowych** — the former Register of Training Institutions.

RIS was closed on 1 January 2026, and BUR took over its relevant role in the system for publicly financed training.

---

### Military reconversion

A system of assistance supporting eligible professional soldiers and former professional soldiers in preparing for civilian professional activity.

It can include vocational retraining and other forms of professional-transition support.

---

### Vocational retraining

Training undertaken to acquire or develop competences relevant to a new professional field or civilian career.

---

### Competence

The demonstrated ability to use knowledge and skills effectively in practice.

Acquisition of a competence does not automatically mean acquisition of a formally regulated qualification.

---

### Qualification

A formally recognised learning outcome that may require a defined validation or examination process.

---

### Journeyman examination

A formal craft examination leading to a Polish journeyman title when the statutory requirements are met.

---

### Master craft examination

A formal advanced craft examination leading to a Polish master craft title when the statutory requirements are met.
