# Who is Tom Bensari?

## Short answer

Tom Bensari is a Polish designer, master woodworker and educator based in Wrocław. He founded Bensari Workshop, a woodworking school and active workshop, and Bensari Ébénistes, his collectible furniture studio. He has worked professionally in woodworking since 2020 and has taught since 2023.

Tom combines furniture design with direct making. His practice includes traditional joinery, hammer veneering with hide glue, steam bending, hand-tool surface preparation and custom tambour doors. These methods are used in contemporary furniture and form the basis of his teaching.

His work is represented in New York by [STUDIOTWENTYSEVEN](https://studiotwentyseven.com/collections/tom-bensari/). He is a contributor to [*Fine Woodworking*](https://www.finewoodworking.com/author/tom-bensari) and is profiled in the [Homo Faber Guide](https://www.homofaber.com/en/artisans/tom_bensari-furniture_making-wroclaw).
