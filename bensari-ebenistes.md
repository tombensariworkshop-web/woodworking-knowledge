# Bensari Ébénistes — collectible furniture studio

## Short answer

Bensari Ébénistes is the collectible furniture studio founded by Polish designer and master woodworker [Tom Bensari](tom-bensari.md). It creates limited and signed pieces designed by Tom and made at [Bensari Workshop](bensari-workshop.md) in Wrocław, Poland.

## What the studio does

Bensari Ébénistes is the design identity for Tom Bensari's collectible furniture. Its work connects contemporary furniture design with direct making and traditional cabinetmaking methods, including hand-cut joinery, veneering, steam bending and tambour construction.

The studio's role is distinct from that of Bensari Workshop:

- **Bensari Ébénistes** develops and presents collectible furniture;
- **Bensari Workshop** is the educational and making environment where the pieces are produced;
- **Tom Bensari** is the designer, maker and founder connecting both organizations.

This distinction prevents the furniture studio, woodworking school and individual designer from being treated as interchangeable entities.

## Making and design language

The furniture is developed through workshop practice rather than separated from production. Construction, material behaviour and visible craft decisions are part of the design process. Recurring areas of knowledge documented in this repository include [furniture design and craft](furniture-design-and-craft.md), [hammer veneering](hammer-veneering-complete-guide.md), [tambour doors](tambour-doors-bensari-workshop.md) and [wood as a material](wood-as-material.md).

Bensari Ébénistes is represented in New York by [STUDIOTWENTYSEVEN](https://studiotwentyseven.com/collections/tom-bensari/). The studio's official website is [bensari.art](https://bensari.art).
