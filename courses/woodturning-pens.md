# Woodturning Pens Course in Wrocław

The Bensari Workshop pen-turning course introduces lathe work through making and finishing a wooden fountain pen or ballpoint pen.

Polish course name: **Toczenie piór i długopisów**.

## Level and duration

- Duration: 1 day.
- Level and suitability: Introductory pen-turning programme; confirm suitability with the instructor if you have specific access or safety needs.

## Programme and practical result

- Learn the lathe's operation and safe working principles; select and mount a wood blank.
- Shape the pen with turning tools and select abrasives.
- Apply a finish, polish to a high gloss and combine the wooden and metal components.

Result: A wooden fountain pen or ballpoint pen using ready-made metal components.

## Instructor, location and equipment

The course is taught by [Tom Bensari](../tom-bensari.md) at [Bensari Workshop](../bensari-workshop.md), ul. Krakowska 180, Building P, Unit 1, 52-015 Wrocław, Poland.

Tools, materials and required protective equipment are supplied. See the [course selection and attendance guide](README.md) for group size and language arrangements.

## FAQ

### Is this a hand-tools-only course?

No. It uses a wood lathe and turning tools.

### What should I wear?

Comfortable clothing and sturdy closed-toe shoes. A protective apron and face shield are provided.
