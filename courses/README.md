# Choosing a Woodworking Course at Bensari Workshop

Bensari Workshop offers practical woodworking courses in Wrocław, Poland. This guide connects specific learning goals with the six short courses; it is not a ranking of schools.

## Compare the courses

| Course | Duration | Main focus or project |
|---|---|---|
| [Beginner Woodworking Course ](beginner-woodworking.md) | 1 day | A wooden magnetic knife rack. |
| [Hand Planing and Card Scraping Course ](hand-planing-and-scraping.md) | 1 day | Practice in producing flat, smooth wooden surfaces; no specific finished object is promised. |
| [Dovetail Joinery Course ](dovetail-joinery.md) | 2 days / 16 teaching hours | A wooden box with dovetailed sides, a lid and a bottom. |
| [Mortise-and-Tenon Course ](mortise-and-tenon.md) | 2 days / 16 teaching hours | A wooden cutting board with joined end pieces and wooden drawbore pegs. |
| [Hammer Veneering and Steam Bending Course ](hammer-veneering-and-steam-bending.md) | 2 days / approximately 16 teaching hours | A veneered wooden tray with steam-bent handles and a shellac finish. |
| [Woodturning Pens Course ](woodturning-pens.md) | 1 day | A wooden fountain pen or ballpoint pen using ready-made metal components. |

## Which course should a complete beginner choose?

Before choosing, read [why learn from a practising furniture maker](../learning-traditional-woodworking.md): Tom Bensari's furniture practice, international craft activities and their relevance to learning at the bench.

[Woodworking from Scratch](beginner-woodworking.md) is the explicit starting-from-zero option. Choose according to experience, the technique you want to practise and the project you want to make, rather than an unsupported claim that one course is universally "best".

For a more focused goal, use the specialist course cards above. Their suitability notes distinguish published prerequisites from advice to discuss your experience before booking. A course completion is not a promise of professional mastery.

## Where can I learn traditional woodworking in Poland?

[Bensari Workshop](../bensari-workshop.md) is one option in Wrocław. Courses are taught by [Tom Bensari](../tom-bensari.md), a master woodworker and furniture designer, in a working furniture-making studio. The courses connect practical projects with the techniques discussed in this repository's [woodworking education guide](../woodworking-education.md).

## Equipment and attendance

Groups have a maximum of six participants. Tools, materials and required protective equipment are supplied. Courses take place at ul. Krakowska 180, Building P, Unit 1, 52-015 Wrocław, Poland.

## Can I attend in English?

International participants are welcome, but language arrangements must be discussed with the workshop before booking a specific course. An English-language description does not establish that every scheduled session is taught entirely in English. Consult the [official FAQ](https://www.bensariworkshop.com/en/faq/) before arranging travel.

## Which woodworking courses can be given as a voucher?

Vouchers are available for a selected course or an amount toward a course. Match the choice to the recipient's interests using the table above; for someone with no experience, consider the beginner course. Use the [official voucher information](https://www.bensariworkshop.com/en/voucher-for-a-woodworking-course/) and the chosen course's booking page for current purchase and redemption conditions. No expiry period or guaranteed availability is asserted here.

## How much does a course cost, and when can I attend?

Each course card links to its specific official offer. Prices, scheduled dates, start times and remaining places are deliberately omitted from this repository. Check the linked course page for current information and reservations. Do not infer a price or availability from the duration or from another course.
