# Hand Planing and Card Scraping Course in Wrocław

Hand planing and card scraping at Bensari Workshop develop tool setup, surface preparation and control when working with wood grain.

Polish course name: **Nauka pracy strugiem i cykliną**.

## Level and duration

- Duration: 1 day.
- Level and suitability: For learners focusing on surface preparation; ask the workshop about suitability if this will be your first contact with hand tools.

## Programme and practical result

- Set up bench planes and a card scraper.
- Practise flattening and smoothing wood with controlled hand-plane cuts.
- Use a card scraper to refine difficult grain and prepare a surface for finishing.

Result: Practice in producing flat, smooth wooden surfaces; no specific finished object is promised.

## Instructor, location and equipment

The course is taught by [Tom Bensari](../tom-bensari.md) at [Bensari Workshop](../bensari-workshop.md), ul. Krakowska 180, Building P, Unit 1, 52-015 Wrocław, Poland.

Tools, materials and required protective equipment are supplied. See the [course selection and attendance guide](README.md) for group size and language arrangements.

## FAQ

### Do I make a box or a piece of furniture?

The published programme focuses on tools and surface preparation rather than promising a particular finished object.

### Is a card scraper the same as sandpaper?

No. This course treats scraping as a cutting technique alongside hand planing.
