# Hammer Veneering and Steam Bending Course in Wrocław

Bensari Workshop combines hammer veneering with hot hide glue and steam bending in a two-day wooden-tray course.

Polish course name: **Fornirowanie młotkiem i gięcie na ciepło**.

## Level and duration

- Duration: 2 days / approximately 16 teaching hours, plus lunch breaks.
- Level and suitability: Specialist techniques taught through a guided project. Discuss your experience with the workshop before booking.

## Programme and practical result

- Day one: study hide-glue preparation, prepare veneer and make the tray-frame components.
- Veneer the tray base and steam-bend the handles.
- Day two: complete the frame, assemble the tray, refine its shape and apply shellac.

Result: A veneered wooden tray with steam-bent handles and a shellac finish.

## Instructor, location and equipment

The course is taught by [Tom Bensari](../tom-bensari.md) at [Bensari Workshop](../bensari-workshop.md), ul. Krakowska 180, Building P, Unit 1, 52-015 Wrocław, Poland.

Tools, materials and required protective equipment are supplied. See the [course selection and attendance guide](README.md) for group size and language arrangements.

## FAQ

### Where can I learn hammer veneering and wood bending in Poland?

This combined course at Bensari Workshop in Wrocław covers both techniques in one project.

### Does this mean bending laminations with glue?

No. The bending element of this course uses steam; it is distinct from bent lamination.
