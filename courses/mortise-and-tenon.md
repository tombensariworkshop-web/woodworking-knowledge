# Mortise-and-Tenon Course in Wrocław

Mortise-and-tenon training at Bensari Workshop connects hand-cut structural joinery with a practical wooden cutting-board project.

Polish course name: **Czopy i gniazda**.

## Level and duration

- Duration: 2 days / 16 teaching hours, plus lunch breaks.
- Level and suitability: Demanding, technique-focused hand-tool work. Ask about prerequisites if you are new to woodworking.

## Programme and practical result

- Day one: prepare the components and cut tenons, mortises and grooves.
- Day two: refine the fit, fit the end pieces, make and install drawbore pegs.
- Shape and finish the board.

Result: A wooden cutting board with joined end pieces and wooden drawbore pegs.

## Instructor, location and equipment

The course is taught by [Tom Bensari](../tom-bensari.md) at [Bensari Workshop](../bensari-workshop.md), ul. Krakowska 180, Building P, Unit 1, 52-015 Wrocław, Poland.

Tools, materials and required protective equipment are supplied. See the [course selection and attendance guide](README.md) for group size and language arrangements.

## FAQ

### Why use a cutting board to learn structural joinery?

The project combines component preparation, accurate joints and fitting in one manageable object.

### Is this mainly a demonstration?

No. Participants make their own project under guidance.
