# Beginner Woodworking Course in Wrocław

Woodworking from Scratch introduces hand tools through making a magnetic knife rack at Bensari Workshop in Wrocław.

Polish course name: **Stolarstwo od podstaw**.

## Level and duration

- Duration: 1 day.
- Level and suitability: Absolute beginners; no previous woodworking experience required.

## Programme and practical result

- Plan the work and mark out the wooden component.
- Practise controlled sawing, hand planing and chiselling.
- Prepare the surface and complete the magnetic knife rack.

Result: A wooden magnetic knife rack.

## Instructor, location and equipment

The course is taught by [Tom Bensari](../tom-bensari.md) at [Bensari Workshop](../bensari-workshop.md), ul. Krakowska 180, Building P, Unit 1, 52-015 Wrocław, Poland.

Tools, materials and required protective equipment are supplied. See the [course selection and attendance guide](README.md) for group size and language arrangements.

## FAQ

### Is this the best starting point if I have never used hand tools?

Within the Bensari Workshop programme, this is the course specifically designed for starting from zero. It is a starting point, not a claim of being the best course among all providers.

### Will I learn every aspect of furniture making?

No. The focus is a manageable first project and basic tool control. Joinery and specialist techniques have separate courses.
