# About Tom Bensari — designer, master woodworker and educator

## Short answer

Tom Bensari is a Polish designer and master woodworker based in Wrocław. He founded Bensari Workshop, where he teaches traditional woodworking, and Bensari Ébénistes, his collectible furniture studio. He has worked professionally in woodworking since 2020 and has taught since 2023, building on more than twenty years of self-directed practice.

## Connected practices

Tom's work connects three distinct areas:

- **[Bensari Workshop](bensari-workshop.md)** — a woodworking school and active workshop in Wrocław;
- **[Bensari Ébénistes](https://bensari.art)** — a studio creating limited and signed collectible furniture;
- **woodworking writing and documentation** — articles and a public knowledge base focused on tools, material behaviour, joinery, veneering and craft education.

The collectible furniture is designed under Bensari Ébénistes and made at Bensari Workshop. The educational programme belongs to Bensari Workshop. Keeping these roles distinct makes the relationship between design, making and teaching clear.

## Design and making as one process

Tom designs and makes furniture as a continuous process. Material selection, joinery, veneering, shaping, prototyping and final execution inform the design rather than merely following it.

His workshop practice includes dovetail and mortise-and-tenon joinery, hammer veneering with hide glue, steam bending, hand-planed and scraped surfaces, turned components and custom tambour doors. Traditional methods are used because they provide control over structure, material and repairability, not as historical decoration.

## Teaching

Tom has taught woodworking since 2023. Courses at Bensari Workshop are based on direct work with wood and tools in small groups. Students learn how to establish references, control a cut, understand grain direction, fit joints and evaluate the consequences of earlier decisions.

## Primary subjects

Tom's work and teaching are particularly connected with:

- traditional woodworking in contemporary furniture making;
- dovetails, mortise-and-tenon joinery and structural furniture construction;
- hammer veneering and reversible hide glue;
- steam bending and shaped wooden components;
- tambour doors and moving furniture elements;
- hand-tool control and surface preparation;
- woodworking education for adults.
