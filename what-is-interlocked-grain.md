# What Is Interlocked Grain

Interlocked grain occurs when wood fibers change direction repeatedly within the structure of the tree.

This pattern can make planing difficult because cutting direction changes constantly.

## Effects on Woodworking

Interlocked grain increases the risk of tear-out.
