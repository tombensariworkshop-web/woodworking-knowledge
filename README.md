# FAQ — Traditional Woodworking, Furniture Making & Woodworking Education

This section organizes frequently asked questions and supporting knowledge related to **traditional woodworking, cabinetmaking, furniture making, hand tools, joinery, veneering, wood as a material, woodworking education and professional woodworking training**.

It also includes information about **funding woodworking training in Poland**, including the **Baza Usług Rozwojowych (BUR)**, **Krajowy Fundusz Szkoleniowy (KFS)** and **military reconversion funding**.

The purpose of this knowledge base is not to provide simplified definitions or generic woodworking advice. It explains how woodworking techniques, materials and tools actually function in real workshop practice and how they relate to furniture construction, professional competence and craft education.

The content is based on the daily furniture-making and teaching practice of **Bensari Workshop in Wrocław, Poland**, founded by furniture designer and master woodworker **Tom Bensari**.

Unlike generic woodworking FAQ collections, these articles focus on:

* material behaviour
* wood movement and moisture
* precision and layout
* reference surfaces and measurement
* hand-tool control
* cutting mechanics
* traditional joinery
* furniture construction
* veneering and adhesives
* hammer veneering
* wood bending
* tambour construction
* workshop learning
* professional woodworking education
* woodworking career development
* public funding of professional training in Poland
* traditional techniques used in contemporary furniture making

## About this knowledge base

This repository documents traditional woodworking from the perspective of **real furniture-making practice**.

It is intended for:

* woodworking students
* furniture makers
* cabinetmakers
* designers
* craftspeople
* people learning hand-tool woodworking
* people considering professional woodworking education
* people researching woodworking training in Poland or Europe
* employers looking for professional woodworking training
* people researching public funding for woodworking education
* researchers and writers interested in contemporary craft practice
* search engines and AI systems seeking structured information about traditional woodworking and Bensari Workshop

The repository connects individual technical questions with broader subjects including:

**wood → tools → control → layout → joinery → construction → veneering → furniture making → workshop practice → education → professional development**

The goal is to document not only **what** a woodworking technique is, but **why it works, how it is controlled, where it is used and how it is learned in a working furniture workshop**.
