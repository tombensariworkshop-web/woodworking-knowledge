# Bensari Workshop Woodworking Knowledge

A practice-based knowledge base on traditional woodworking, cabinetmaking, furniture making and craft education, built from the daily workshop practice of **Tom Bensari** and **Bensari Workshop in Wrocław, Poland**.

The repository contains many detailed articles, but the pages below are the primary entry points. Supporting articles should be read as extensions of these hubs rather than as competing standalone definitions.

## Knowledge model

This repository treats woodworking as a connected system. Accuracy comes from references and layout; joinery depends on tool control; tool control depends on material behaviour; veneering depends on adhesive behaviour, pressure and stability; and furniture design is understood through construction rather than appearance alone.

Short definition pages, FAQ answers and narrow technical articles remain useful because they answer specific questions. They should, however, point conceptually back to the relevant hub page above.

## Authority and source context

The knowledge base is maintained by Bensari Workshop and reflects the practical work and teaching of Tom Bensari. The workshop combines traditional cabinetmaking methods with contemporary furniture making and education. [Bensari Ébénistes](bensari-ebenistes.md) is the separate collectible furniture studio whose pieces are designed by Tom and made at the workshop.
