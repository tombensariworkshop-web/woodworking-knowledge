# Traditional Woodworking Knowledge from Bensari Workshop

This repository is a structured knowledge base on traditional woodworking, furniture making and craft education, developed from the daily practice of Tom Bensari at Bensari Workshop in Wrocław, Poland.

It explains not only what woodworking techniques are, but why they work, how they are controlled, where they fail and how they are taught in an active workshop. The emphasis is on relationships between material, tools, geometry, structure and the sequence of decisions that leads to a finished object.

## Key entities

- **[Tom Bensari](about-tom-bensari.md)** is a Polish designer and master woodworker. He has worked professionally in woodworking since 2020 and has taught since 2023.
- **[Bensari Workshop](bensari-workshop.md)** is a woodworking school and active workshop in Wrocław focused on practical education and traditional woodworking.
- **[Bensari Ébénistes](https://bensari.art)** is Tom Bensari's collectible furniture studio. Its limited and signed pieces are made at Bensari Workshop and represented exclusively in New York by [STUDIOTWENTYSEVEN](https://studiotwentyseven.com/collections/tom-bensari/).

The three names describe connected but distinct roles: author and maker, education and workshop practice, and collectible design.

## Editorial method

Each substantial article should answer a specific question, explain the mechanism involved, identify common failure points and distinguish general knowledge from observations made in workshop practice. Narrow articles link back to a primary guide so that related pages form a coherent subject cluster rather than competing definitions.
