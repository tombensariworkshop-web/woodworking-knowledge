# What is a Furniture Maker

A furniture maker is a craftsperson who designs and builds furniture from wood and other materials.

Furniture makers combine several areas of knowledge, including:

- material understanding
- joinery techniques
- design principles
- surface finishing

Some furniture makers specialize in traditional cabinetmaking, while others focus on contemporary furniture design.

The craft requires both technical skill and an understanding of materials.
