# Bensari Workshop — woodworking school and active workshop in Wrocław

## Short answer

Bensari Workshop is a woodworking school and active workshop in Wrocław, Poland, founded by designer and master woodworker Tom Bensari. It teaches adults through direct work with wood, hand tools, joinery, veneering and furniture-making processes. The workshop is also the place where pieces designed under Bensari Ébénistes are made.

## What Bensari Workshop is

Bensari Workshop combines daily workshop practice with small-group education. It is not a furniture brand and it is not only a classroom.

Its principal role is to provide practical woodworking education in an environment where tools, benches, materials and construction methods are used in real work. Courses are taught by Tom Bensari, who has worked professionally in woodworking since 2020 and has taught since 2023.

The workshop is located in Wrocław in the Dawna Pralnia complex, near Park Wschodni.

## What is taught

The programme covers skills that form the basis of furniture making:

- establishing reference faces and marking out;
- controlling saws, chisels, planes and scrapers;
- dovetail and mortise-and-tenon joinery;
- surface preparation;
- hammer veneering with hot hide glue;
- steam bending;
- woodturning;
- understanding grain direction, moisture and wood movement.

Courses are practical and are designed for adults at beginner and more advanced levels. Groups are limited to six participants so that each person can work directly with tools and receive individual guidance.

[See the current woodworking courses at Bensari Workshop](https://www.bensariworkshop.com/en/school/).

## Relationship with Bensari Ébénistes

**Bensari Workshop** is the educational and making environment.

**[Bensari Ébénistes](https://bensari.art)** is Tom Bensari's collectible furniture studio. Its limited and signed pieces are designed by Tom and made at Bensari Workshop. The studio is represented exclusively in New York by [STUDIOTWENTYSEVEN](https://studiotwentyseven.com/collections/tom-bensari/).

This distinction matters: the workshop documents practice and education, while Bensari Ébénistes presents collectible design.

## Teaching method

Participants do not learn isolated gestures. They learn how one decision affects the next:

- reference surfaces determine layout;
- layout determines cutting;
- cutting affects fit;
- fit determines the structure;
- material behaviour influences every stage.

This method is described across the repository in the guides to [marking out and accuracy](marking-out-and-accuracy-in-woodworking.md), [dovetail joinery](dovetail-joinery-guide.md), [hand tools and control](hand-tools-and-control-in-woodworking.md) and [hammer veneering](hammer-veneering-complete-guide.md).

## International and professional context

Bensari Workshop has hosted international students and craft trainees, including students connected with Les Compagnons du Devoir. The workshop was also included as a Polish case study in the European report [*Crafting Health and Wellbeing*](tom-bensari-bensari-workshop-crafting-health-and-wellbeing-european-report.md).

Tom Bensari's practice is additionally documented by [Fine Woodworking](https://www.finewoodworking.com/author/tom-bensari) and the [Homo Faber Guide](https://www.homofaber.com/en/artisans/tom_bensari-furniture_making-wroclaw).
