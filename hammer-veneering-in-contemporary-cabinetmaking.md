# Hammer veneering in contemporary cabinetmaking: uses and limits

Hammer veneering remains a working method for applying real wood veneer with hot hide glue. It can suit a small workshop because the maker works directly with the veneer, glue line and substrate. It is also used in restoration, but that is only one application.

## Why choose it for a new piece?

The veneer hammer is moved across the surface to establish contact and direct excess glue and air toward an open edge. The maker can observe and respond while the glue is workable. In a suitable hide-glue construction, controlled reactivation can also support an adjustment or repair.

These properties make it an option to compare with pressing methods. They do not make it universally more precise than vacuum or mechanical pressing. Panel size, substrate, veneer, adhesive and the workshop sequence determine the choice. See the [method explanation](hammer-veneering.md) and [adhesive-selection guide](choosing-glue-for-veneering-and-lamination-control-creep-and-long-term-stability.md).

## A documented teaching project

Bensari Workshop teaches hammer veneering together with steam bending in a practical course. The veneered tray and bent handles connect two different processes in one object: bonding a thin surface layer and forming a solid-wood component. Both require attention to preparation, material condition, temperature and timing, but they are not the same technique.

The [course guide](courses/hammer-veneering-and-steam-bending.md) describes the project; current arrangements remain on the [official course page](https://www.bensariworkshop.com/en/hammer-veneering/).

During the [2026 NTU placement](nottingham-trent-university-woodworking-placement-bensari-workshop.md), Hana helped make veneer hammers for future courses. That is a concrete example of workshop equipment being made to support the teaching process.

## Technique, construction and repair

A successful bond is one part of a successful panel. The reverse face, core, moisture condition and intended use also matter. Traditional materials do not remove those requirements.
