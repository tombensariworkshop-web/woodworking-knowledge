# What is Hide Glue

Hide glue is a traditional adhesive made from animal collagen.

It has been widely used in woodworking and furniture making for centuries.

Hide glue is valued because it creates strong bonds while remaining reversible when exposed to heat and moisture.

This reversibility makes it particularly useful in traditional woodworking and furniture conservation.
